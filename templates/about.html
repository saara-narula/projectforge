Collaborative & Content-Based Hybrid Engine

ProjectForge simultaneously computes Content-Based Filtering and Collaborative Filtering into a unified 50/50 ensemble to maximize both personal skill relevance and community peer validation.

1. Content-Based Filtering (50% Weight)

Evaluates the intrinsic properties, prerequisites, and semantic relevance of each project against your selected profile:

Domain Alignment (25%)

Matches your target industry fields (AI, Web, Cyber, Robotics, FinTech).

Tech Stack Jaccard (25%)

Calculates overlap with your existing skills while identifying growth skills.

Academic Discipline (18%)

Validates major compatibility heuristics and degree prerequisites.

Difficulty & Scope (17%)

Matches experience level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced) and timeline.

TF-IDF Semantics (15%)

Cosine similarity over term-frequency vectors on custom interest keywords.

2. Collaborative Filtering (50% Weight)

Harnesses collective community wisdom from peer ratings and student interaction patterns:

👥 User-Based Peer Cohorts

Groups students by degree program and prior project ratings. If students in your major rated a project 5 stars, it receives an elevated cohort recommendation score.

🔗 Item-Item Co-Rating Matrix

Computes pairwise cosine similarity between project rating vectors. Powers the "Students who completed this project also loved..." section on every project page.

Why Combining Collaborative & Content-Based Filtering Wins

Pure collaborative filtering struggles with the Cold-Start Problem (unrated projects are never recommended). Meanwhile, pure content-based filtering suffers from over-specialization and cannot gauge whether students actually enjoyed building the project.

By executing both models in parallel and fusing their normalized scores into a unified Hybrid Ensemble, ProjectForge ensures every suggested project has the exact technical depth you need while bubbling up the most rewarding, peer-approved projects.
